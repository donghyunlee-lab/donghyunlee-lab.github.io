We are pleased to announce two new appointments for **Professor Donghyun Lee**.

- **Chair, AI & Digital Committee, Korea Environmental Policy and Administration Society (KEPAS)** — term from August 31, 2026 to February 28, 2028. In this role, Professor Lee will lead the society's discussions on how AI and digital technologies can support environmental policy research.
- **Member, Pre-Review Committee for the K-SDGs Implementation Plan, National Sustainable Development Research Center** — term from September 1, 2026 to August 31, 2028. The committee reviews draft implementation plans for the Korean Sustainable Development Goals (K-SDGs).

Both appointments connect our lab's work on trustworthy AI for environmental forecasting with the policy communities that use it. Congratulations! 🎉
