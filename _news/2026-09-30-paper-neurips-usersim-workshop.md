🎉 Our paper, "From Next-Action Fidelity to Rollout Validity: A Human-Grounded Audit of LLM User Simulators in Repeated Social Interaction," has been accepted at the **NeurIPS 2026 Workshop on Grounded User Simulation for Model Evaluation and Training (UserSim)**. All three reviewers recommended acceptance.

The paper was written by **Hyeongseo Jeon** (first author, M.S. student) and **Prof. Donghyun Lee** (corresponding). It checks whether LLMs that stand in for people still behave like people over many rounds of social interaction. Congratulations, Hyeongseo! 🎊

> **Non-archival:** UserSim is a non-archival workshop. The paper is not part of the NeurIPS proceedings, and the authors keep the right to publish a full version elsewhere. The workshop is held in Paris on December 12, 2026.

---

**Citation**: Jeon, H., & Lee, D. (2026). From next-action fidelity to rollout validity: A human-grounded audit of LLM user simulators in repeated social interaction. _NeurIPS 2026 Workshop on Grounded User Simulation for Model Evaluation and Training (UserSim)_. Non-archival.
