<p class="rp-lead">
  Floods and wildfires hit people, ecosystems, and infrastructure at the same time. This project
  combines physics-based models with AI to build disaster forecasts that are fast enough for
  time-critical response and reliable enough to act on. We call this view Disaster One Health
  Informatics.
</p>

<h2 class="h4 mt-4">Research directions</h2>
<ul class="rp-directions">
  <li>
    <h3>Physics-AI hybrid models</h3>
    <p>Deep generative models that keep to physical laws.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <h3>MambaFlow matching</h3>
    <p>Efficient sequence modeling for disaster simulation and forecasting.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <h3>Real-time prediction</h3>
    <p>Forecasting systems for time-critical disaster scenarios.</p>
  </li>
</ul>
