<p class="rp-lead">
  Algal bloom warnings help water plants act early, but observations are often sparse. This
  project builds one framework that joins physics-based models with dynamic graph meta-learning,
  so that forecasts of harmful algal blooms stay accurate and reliable where data are scarce.
</p>

<h2 class="h4 mt-4">Research directions</h2>
<ul class="rp-directions">
  <li>
    <h3>Physics and AI together</h3>
    <p>Physics-based models and deep learning combined in one forecasting framework.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <h3>Dynamic graph meta-learning</h3>
    <p>Learning designed to make the most of sparse observations.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <h3>Reliable forecasts</h3>
    <p>The aim: accurate, reliable forecasts of harmful algal blooms.</p>
  </li>
</ul>
